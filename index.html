When relentless suffering assaults us, encouragement should not sound like another cliché.

Whether you are facing cancer, grief, chronic illness, disability, addiction, trauma, or another season of deep hardship, you don't need simplistic answers to impossible questions. You need hope that is honest.

The Intrusion of Suffering – Pursuing Resilience in the Midst of Adversity was written for people who are exhausted—not just physically, but emotionally and spiritually. Drawing from my own journey through having a special needs child; another child with trauma-induced mental illness, including multiple addictions, overdoses, and incarceration; a spouse with cancer and chronic pain; my own journey overcoming suicidal ideation and now coping with Stage 4 metastatic cancer, this book offers practical encouragement without minimizing pain. It also provides enduring hope found in Scripture.

Today I serve patients and families as a professional trauma chaplain, actively working at three hospitals. I have years of walking alongside patients and families during the climax of their crisis, including life altering injuries, complex pediatric care, behavioral health, and suicide. Combining my season of church ministry with spiritual care in the hospital setting, I have responded to well over 1,000 deaths (including pediatric loss) and surpassed 1,000 trauma one incidents.

Each chapter in The Intrusion of Suffering begins with a poem written during my own experience with cancer and explores a biblical theme that speaks to real suffering. The chapters are intentionally brief, ending with questions for reflection and resources to encourage you in your own journey.

My prayer is simple: that you would discover you are not alone, that suffering does not have the final word, and that hope can endure even in life's darkest seasons.
